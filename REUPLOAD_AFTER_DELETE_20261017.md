Product deletion uses the physical-delete route implemented in services/productPermanentDelete.js. Removed products no longer appear in products or product_variants, and barcode mappings are released. The original Excel can be uploaded again after deleting the matching records, subject to duplicate barcode and import validation checks. For previously soft-deleted CHARAK/ESSA records use ONE_TIME_CHARAK_ESSA_PERMANENT_CLEANUP.sql after a full backup and dry run.
